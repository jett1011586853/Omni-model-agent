# NotebookLM Skill

## Use when
- You are summarizing documents, research, or source-backed material.

## Quick rules
- Ground every claim in a source.
- Separate what the source says from your inference.
- Highlight contradictions and missing evidence.
- Prefer question-driven synthesis over free-form summary.

## Good habits
- Keep a short source map.
- Make it obvious when the answer is incomplete.
